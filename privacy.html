Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Amupys (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates this website to provide information about our Android applications, links to official app listings, application screenshots, release information, support options, and APK download links when available. We value user privacy and aim to keep the website simple and transparent.

1. Information collected by this website

The Amupys website does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not ask for passwords, payment details, or private account credentials through the website.

If you use the support form, you may enter your name, email address, selected application, subject, and message. The current support form opens your configured email application using a mailto link. The website itself does not store the message on an Amupys server.
